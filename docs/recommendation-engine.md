# ACCORDAI AI Recommendation Engine

## Purpose

ACCORDAI Recommendation Engine provides intelligent insurance recommendations for agency operations.

## Recommendation Types

- Cross-sell recommendations
- Renewal risk recommendations
- Fraud risk recommendations
- Underwriting review recommendations
- Customer opportunity scoring
- Producer follow-up recommendations

## Current Rule-Based Signals

### Customer Signals

- total premium
- policy mix
- missing coverage types
- account value

### Policy Signals

- premium level
- claim history
- renewal concerns

### Claim Signals

- reserve amount
- prior claim activity
- fraud indicators

## Future Enhancements

- machine learning risk scoring
- predictive renewal models
- customer lifetime value scoring
- producer productivity analytics
- personalized cross-sell recommendations
- AI-driven next best action engine
