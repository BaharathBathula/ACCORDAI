- Backend architecture
- PostgreSQL models

- Customer management
- Policy management
- Claims management

- Multi-tenancy
- RBAC permissions
- Audit logging
- Tenant isolation
- Enterprise governance

## Phase 5 — Advanced AI
- LangGraph orchestration
- Multi-agent systems
- AI workflow automation
- RAG architecture
- Predictive underwriting
- Autonomous claims workflows

- Kubernetes deployment
- Redis caching
- Celery workers
- Observability stack

- Monitoring and alerting

ACCORDAI aims to become a fully AI-native insurance operating system capable of modernizing insurance agency operations through intelligent automation and conversational AI.
